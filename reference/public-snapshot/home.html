Kuki. Baked fresh, served warm.

Six flavours, each made from scratch in small batches with real ingredients and no shortcuts. Blueberry, Masala, Nutella Chunk, Rose Gulkand, Boost and Omam, every one of them portioned, shaped and baked through the day.

FRESHLY BAKED

The Finest Ingredients

Crafted from the best, because extraordinary cookies deserve nothing less.

Handcrafted in Small Batches

Every ball of dough is portioned and shaped by hand, and baked in small trays through the day. It is slower, but it is the only way to get the texture right.

Natural Ingredients Only

Real butter, real fruit, real spices. Our fillings are never substitutes, from the blueberry to the gulkand to the Nutella chunks.

Blueberry, Masala, Nutella Chunk, Rose Gulkand, Boost and Omam. Each one earns its place on the tray.
