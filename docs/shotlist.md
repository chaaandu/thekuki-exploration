# Photo shot list

The site works fully without these, using illustrated jars, cookie art and the existing
product photos. Real photos make it warmer. Every shot belongs to one world: an old
neighbourhood bakery counter in afternoon light. Use real objects and window light, with
no props from other brands (Boost, Nutella and so on can be named in text only).

Shoot on a phone if needed: 4:5 or 1:1, natural light from one side, no filters.

## Alt text

Write what someone would see, not what we want them to feel:

- Good: "Masala cookies on brown paper, curry leaves pressed into the crust"
- Not: "Delicious handmade masala cookies made with love"

Decorative images (illustrated jars, aroma, textures that repeat the caption) are already
marked `alt=""` in the theme. Product photos use the alt text set in Shopify admin
(Products › media › alt text). Fill it for every photo.
